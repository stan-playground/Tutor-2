## Prioritized findings

**Blocking findings: none.**

1. **Learner evidence and numeric labels are source-consistent.** The guide separates arithmetic fixtures from response-level extraction fixtures, so authored opportunity expectations do not become observations without actual responses. The example gives the correct separate rates: 6/8 = 0.75, 5/10 = 0.50 and 3/4 = 0.75. Required fixtures explicitly cover zero denominators, two/three observations, four/five opportunities, exact ±0.15 changes, stable results, 0.85 maintenance qualifications, latest-four/90-day selection, mixed evidence and corrected duplicates. Recognition/assistance/task distinctions, uncertainty, alternatives and completion-versus-ability remain visible. No old automatic-mastery or historical completion threshold was reintroduced.

2. **The twelve families have concrete and distinct coverage.** The matrix contains four A2, four B1 and four B2 families, with six ordinary and six critical base cases. It covers supported practice/maintenance, domain adaptation/required use, overuse/avoidance/open responses, unknown/stale evidence, conflicting evidence and exhausted useful clarification, missing eligible assets, safety/adult eligibility, tenant/privacy, answer validity/key isolation, instruction-like transcript text, rejection/stale action and substantive duration revision/repeated finalization. The missing-adult, wrong-key and ambiguous-key variants are explicit rather than implied by base names.

3. **Stopped cases do not manufacture stage coverage.** HW-05 and HW-06 have meaningful incomplete outcomes; a missing-adult variant stops earlier. The item contract and stage-membership manifest require expected reached stages and reasons for unreached stages. All active boundaries have fixed input and expected-label definitions, ordinary-success and relevant critical-case requirements, supplemented by isolated fixtures where needed. It is correct not to force every complete-job family through every graph node.

4. **Generation stays within the homework pilot.** The asset/adaptation criteria preserve the exact learning mechanism, reviewed provenance, level and constraints. No-asset cases cannot pass through invented references, silent objective/context relaxation or unrelated original exercise authoring. Open speaking tasks use assessment criteria; valid alternative answers cannot fail solely for differing from reference prose. Other preparation jobs remain deferred.

5. **Review and output assertions preserve teaching responsibility.** Key solving is independent of the proposed key, unsupported/unsafe/ambiguous content blocks readiness, substantive revision freezes learner evidence and invalidates affected approvals, stale decisions leave state unchanged, and approved export excludes rejected/unapproved material and tutor-only keys. These checks observe actual persistence/export/provider boundaries when implemented; model acknowledgment alone is insufficient.

6. **Gate arithmetic and evidence truth are explicit.** Six ordinary end-to-end base families require six passes at the 90% threshold; isolated-stage denominators remain separate. Missing/error required scores fail rather than consume an ordinary semantic-failure allowance. Every required critical variant must pass and accepted baseline regressions remain blocking. Gold admission needs item-level qualified review, resolved disagreements and second-review coverage; naming families or approving one variant cannot approve all stage labels. The guide and native draft agree that these are construction requirements, not already-created gold or passed experiments.

7. **Judge calibration avoids brittle or leaked targets.** The two datasets have distinct purposes; candidate task/solver/judge payloads withhold the relevant labels/keys/verdicts. Criterion-based accepted alternatives replace exact prose matching for open outputs. At least 20 distinct human-labeled outputs cannot be faked by repeated criteria on fewer outputs, related variants stay together across split boundaries, both error directions are inspected and tuning consumes the independence of the old holdout. No reliability or learning-gain claim follows from the count.
