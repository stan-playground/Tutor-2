## Owner handoff to reviewers

The owner previously confirmed that reviewers 1 and 2 were qualified and
available for E0. Their assignment and access for this new content review are
not yet confirmed. Once arranged, send each reviewer the **keyless selection**
and request the following independently, without sharing the other reviewer's
answers:

1. Check the file identity and hash above. Solve both choices before opening
   the linked source or seeing its proposed answers. Record the answers,
   reasoning, any ambiguity and whether a key or another review was already
   seen; prior exposure means the solve was not blind.
2. Then compare with the source's answers and assess the objective, B1 level,
   instructions, time context, correctness, valid alternatives, accessibility
   and safety for adult learners. Say whether the two items work as a
   general-domain source for adaptation to software-project updates. Identify
   any needed changes and whether they require another review.
3. Return a separate `approve`, `revise` or `reject` decision for this exact
   source selection, with reviewer ID, qualification, date and reasons. The
   owner reconciles disagreements and records their resolution; an amended
   selection gets a new identity and review. Do not claim blind independence
   when the reviewer has seen the proposed key.

access or assignment for this selection, and a review of an E0 case does not
