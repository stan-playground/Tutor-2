# Independent review of expanded E0 drafts

Date: 4 November 2026. An independent agent checked the expanded numeric,
window, feedback and contrast draft items against PD-08/09, the dataset contract,
current implementation, v4 test reports and recorded input hashes. This is not
qualified-tutor review or human acceptance.

The reviewer found two ambiguities: the original contrast exercise could admit
a past-simple alternative for an open period, and the FB-02 accuracy measure did
not identify which form was counted as the target. The exercise was revised to
specify an action still continuing since Monday, and FB-02 now identifies
present perfect as the counted form. The affected unit and integration checks
were rerun and recorded in [E0 checks](../e0-checks.md).

Final verdict: **GO for the expanded draft preparation and local evidence**.
The reviewer checked the numeric thresholds and selected sources, feedback
candidate and ownership facts, draft status, the contrast limitation, v4
reports and input hashes. This verdict does not approve pedagogical labels,
full E0, or the unobserved exclusion of a positive-form-only activity from an
eligible corpus. It does not review or approve a human-review workbook; the
workbook, including a keyless exercise view, needs its own verification.
